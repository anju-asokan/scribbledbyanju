Experiential Marketing: Live Engraving & Calligraphy for Product Launches in Dublin

Shoppers scroll past static displays. A live personalisation station stops them — a professional artist engraving or hand-lettering products on-site, in front of a crowd, using a precision micro-drill or pen. For retail pop-ups, product launches and PR activations, this is experiential marketing that earns dwell time, footfall and the kind of organic social content paid media can't buy.

Each personalised piece becomes a walking advertisement: a guest photographs their engraved bottle or hand-lettered card, tags your brand, and shares it with their own audience — turning a single activation into ongoing reach long after the event ends.

Common Questions

Brand Activation FAQs

What is a live engraving or live calligraphy activation?

It's an experiential marketing station where an artist hand-engraves or hand-letters products in front of shoppers and press — turning a product launch or pop-up into a moment that drives footfall, dwell time and social content.

What kind of events do you work on?

Product launches, retail pop-ups, PR activations and in-store brand moments across Dublin and Ireland — anywhere a brand wants footfall, dwell time and earned social content.

How many items can be personalised per hour?

It depends on footfall and design complexity. Simpler personalisation moves faster, while detailed work takes a little longer — we tailor the pace and setup to suit your activation.

How far in advance should we book an activation?

4-6 weeks' notice is ideal to confirm the date and plan logistics, though we're happy to discuss shorter-notice activations where possible.
