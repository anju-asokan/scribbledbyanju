Common Questions

Brand Activation FAQs

What is live calligraphy and hand engraving for brand activations?

It's an on-site personalisation experience where products such as fragrance bottles, beauty items, glassware or gifts are hand-lettered or engraved in front of customers — creating an interactive, memorable moment at product launches, pop-ups and retail events.

What kind of events do you work on?

Product launches, retail pop-ups, PR activations and in-store brand moments across Dublin and Ireland — anywhere a brand wants an engaging, personalised customer experience.

How many items can be personalised per hour?

It depends on footfall and design complexity. Simpler personalisation moves faster, while detailed work takes a little longer — we tailor the pace and setup to suit your activation.

How far in advance should we book an activation?

4-6 weeks' notice is ideal to confirm the date and plan logistics, though we're happy to discuss shorter-notice activations where possible.
