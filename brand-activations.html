Trusted onsite event personalisation for brand activations, product launches, pop-ups and retail activations across Dublin and Ireland — live calligraphy, live engraving and hand lettering that gives every guest a shareable, one-of-a-kind keepsake.

Live event engraving is an experiential marketing and luxury gifting service where a professional artist personalises items on-site using a precision micro-drill. It's a favourite for brand activations, retail pop-ups, corporate galas and high-end weddings — turning an everyday product into a memorable keepsake right in front of your guests. Paired with live calligraphy, this style of interactive artistry has become a go-to way for weddings, corporate functions, brand activations and VIP gifting across Dublin and Ireland to feel personal, premium and genuinely shareable.
