Common Questions

Corporate Event FAQs

What corporate events do you cater for?

PR launches, conferences, corporate galas, product unveilings and VIP client gifting evenings across Dublin and Ireland — anywhere a company wants to leave a personal, professional impression.

Can you personalise branded corporate gifts?

Yes. Pens, glassware, notebooks and other branded merchandise can be hand-lettered or engraved on-site or in-studio ahead of your event.

Do you work with event planners and agencies?

Yes, we regularly collaborate with event planners and PR agencies to deliver personalisation as part of a larger event brief.

How much space is needed for a live station?

A small table and chair is usually all that's required, making it easy to fit into most corporate venues without disrupting the layout.

What services do you provide for corporate events?

We offer live hand engraving on branded gifts, live calligraphy for corporate gifting and hot foiling — along with other bespoke personalisation services tailored to your company and event.

Do you provide the items to be personalised, or do we?

Either works. We can personalise items you supply (such as branded product or packaging), or advise on suitable blanks to source for the activation.

Do you cater for office Christmas parties and corporate Christmas gifting?

Yes. We run live calligraphy and engraving stations for office Christmas parties, client thank-you evenings and end-of-year gifting — personalising baubles, cards, glassware and hampers on the night.

How early should we book a Christmas event or gifting activation?

December books out quickly, so we recommend confirming your Christmas party or gifting programme by October to guarantee your preferred date.
